One alignment, from first glance to the lock.

Nothing here is a maze or a score attack. The puzzle is the gap between clocks. Wider speed differences make the meeting point harder to hit, and the beams keep moving while you decide.

  1. 01

    See the network

    Beacons rotate continuously, each at a speed of its own. Their beams are already in motion when the moment starts.

  2. 02

    Choose a surge

    Tap a beacon to accelerate it for a short window. Timing the tap matters more than tapping often.

  3. 03

    Stack the boosts

    When several beacons must arrive together, fire rapid consecutive boosts before the earlier acceleration ends.

  4. 04

    Trust the marker

    Beam movement and the central convergence indicator update in real time, so you can see the lock forming — or slipping.

The skill

Speed is a tool with an expiry.

A boost is temporary on purpose. You are not setting a new permanent rate. You are borrowing a faster spin long enough for that beam to catch the others at one shared point. Miss the window and the beacon settles back into its own rhythm.

Feedback

Beams stay visible.

You do not guess in the dark. Sweep lines and the center indicator show whether the group is tightening.
